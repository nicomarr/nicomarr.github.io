I offer hands-on training on using Large Language Models for biomedical research, capacity building, and community health, with a special focus on supporting researchers in resource-limited settings. Training is delivered mainly as three-month cohort programs for university departments, research institutes, community organizations, and corporate teams, with shorter guest lecture series available as an introduction.

## LLM Training for Biomedical Researchers and Organizations

### Current and Recent Engagements (2025 & 2026)

- Multi-month course for biomedical researchers at the [Kintampo Health Research Centre](https://kintampo-hrc.org/) (Ghana) and the [Immune Resilience](https://immuneresilience.github.io/) research team at Dalhousie University
- Capacity building training for [No'kmaq Village / Flat Bay Band Inc.](https://flatbayband.ca/), a Mi'kmaq self-governance organization in Newfoundland, Canada
- Guest lectures on LLMs for literature analysis at the [Department of Microbiology & Immunology, Dalhousie University](https://medicine.dal.ca/departments/department-sites/microbiology/education.html) (graduate level)

### Course Topics

- Ethical considerations and responsible use
- Context engineering (prompt engineering) for scientific and professional tasks
- Building reproducible workflows with APIs and Jupyter Notebooks
- Audio transcription and meeting documentation
- Systematic error analysis and evaluation of agentic workflows
- Literature synthesis and document processing with citations
- Data exploration, transformation, and visualization
- Automating information retrieval and building agent skills

### Format Options

- Three-month cohort programs (about 10–12 weeks) with live sessions and virtual office hours
- Optional train-the-trainer, developing local facilitators so capacity stays in the institution
- Guest lectures and seminars (1–3 sessions) as an introduction to the material, rather than a substitute for the full course
- Hands-on exercises using real tasks from your own work
- No programming experience required
- Reduced-cost access for qualifying individuals and institutions

### Availability

I am available for new training engagements starting **October 2026**. [Get in touch](https://nicomarr.github.io/contact/) to discuss how a course could work for your team.
